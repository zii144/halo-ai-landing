Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026 · Applies to Halo AI v1.0

Summary: Conversations are stored on your device. When you send a message, that turn goes to our agent proxy so Claude can reply. The Anthropic API key never lives in the app. Analytics is off by default and never includes chat content.

1. Who we are

Halo AI is developed by Zii Wong ("we," "us"). Questions: quickpolymath@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Conversations — messages and replies in on-device SQLite (native) or a local snapshot store (web).
  • Preferences — interface language and related settings.
  • Debug analytics buffer — only when analytics debug mode is on; in-memory, privacy-filtered, not sent to us by default.

Delete conversations or wipe all local data from Settings. Uninstalling removes local data, subject to platform behavior.

3. Data processed when you chat

Sending a message posts that turn (and needed context) to our agent proxy. The proxy holds the Anthropic key and forwards the request to Claude. The reply streams back and is saved locally. We do not sell chat content.

4. What we do not collect by default

  • No account or sign-in
  • No Anthropic API key on your device
  • No analytics of chat content
  • No bulk export that ships your history off-device

5. Permissions

v1 text chat does not require microphone, camera, or contacts. Future voice/vision modes are not shipping in this release.

6. Children

Halo AI is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the effective date will change when we do. Contact: quickpolymath@gmail.com.
